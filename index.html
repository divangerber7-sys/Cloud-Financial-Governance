Cloud cost chargeback allocates Azure spend to business units based on tagged resource consumption. The chargeback model uses a combination of direct allocation (workloads fully owned by one BU) and shared allocation (platform costs split by usage proportion). Monthly chargeback statements are issued to BU finance leads by the 5th of the following month. Disputed allocations are reviewed by the CDO within 10 business days. Unallocated (untagged) resources are held in a central IT cost pool.
